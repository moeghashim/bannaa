# Banna — Goal & Three Tracks

**Last updated:** August 10, 2026

---

## Mission & Goal

Banna exists to get Arab youth ready for the AI boom.

The Arab world is full of young people — the majority are under 25. Historically, the region has not produced companies at the speed or scale it should have, due to fragmentation, differing legal and government environments, and regional instability. This left a generation stuck using products and solutions built for previous generations.

AI changes the equation. It makes generating products dramatically easier and cheaper. Highly specific products can now be created and shipped by small teams. Funding, which was previously a major barrier, is no longer the primary constraint.

**Core Goal:**  
Help Arab youth build lean, AI-powered companies of **1 to 10 people** that operate with exceptional productivity. These companies should be capable of generating **tens of millions to hundreds of millions of dollars** in revenue.

Instead of the 100–200 startups created in the Arab world over recent decades, Banna aims to enable the creation of **10,000 micro-startups**.

## The Three Tracks

Everything Banna teaches and builds content around falls into three tracks.

### 1. Founder Skill Set

This track prepares the individual who will start and lead the company.

- What it means to be a solo founder in the AI era
- Thinking big while staying lean (1–10 person companies targeting tens or hundreds of millions)
- Solo founder mindset and daily operating system
- Building a highly performative team of under ten people
- Hiring for AI-native companies (skills, culture, speed)
- Defining product requirements when AI can ship features overnight
- Fundraising versus bootstrapping and staying profitable
- Understanding investor incentives versus founder incentives
- Dealing with the board in non-VC-style growth companies
- What happens when growth is real but not “VC acceptable”
- Board pressure to change direction for higher growth rates
- Scaling decision-making as the company grows past the founding team
- Maintaining founder control and vision in small high-output teams

### 2. Building in the Age of AI

This track covers how to actually build products and systems with modern AI tools.

- Speed as a competitive advantage
- Understanding LLMs and how they actually work for builders
- LLM harnesses: what they are and how to use them
- Multiplayer products and real-time collaboration features
- Agent coding: writing code with and for AI agents
- Deployment strategies for AI-powered products
- Local versus remote development and inference
- Continuous update, test, and iterate loops
- Shipping in days instead of months
- Observability and monitoring for agent-based systems
- Cost control and token economics for small teams
- Security and reliability when AI is in the critical path

### 3. Builder Skill Set

This track is for every team member (including the founder). In the age of AI, specialized roles are less valuable than strong generalists.

- The shift from specialized roles to generalist builders
- Why everyone on the team must be good at design
- Writing clear documents and product specs with AI
- Building the product yourself
- Testing the product thoroughly
- Deploying and owning the live system
- Iterating rapidly without burnout
- What changed: the new expected skill set for every team member
- Creating a team culture comfortable with continuous fast change
- Accepting that the product can change significantly every three months
- Accepting that the product can be reinvented entirely every six months
- Preventing burnout in high-velocity AI environments
- How each small team builds and defends its own moat
- Moat types that work for 1–10 person AI companies
- How to choose what to learn next as a generalist
- If you are technical: what business and design skills to learn to cover the gaps
- If you are design-oriented: what technical and business skills to learn to cover the gaps
- If you are business-oriented: what technical and design skills to learn to cover the gaps
- Practical learning paths for each starting point
- Balancing depth versus breadth when time is limited
